# Superseded — the workflow app plan moved

This file briefly held revision 2 of the plan to grow bugarach from a detector
viewer into a full workflow. **Do not build from it.** It went through the
murderboard on the day it was written and did not survive: eleven roles returned
roughly a hundred and fifty findings, including four quantities that read as
measurements and could not be traced to any source in any repository.

Three of its central proposals were wrong rather than merely imprecise. Its fitting
method was the error the tree's own estimator documents and forbids. Its parity
target could not exercise the function its first milestone exists to port. Its
account of which reference copy the analysis actually reads was inverted.

The plan it asked for is here:

- **[`docs/export_folder_spec.md`](../export_folder_spec.md)** — the input contract

same task on the same day, and a reader who follows a reference to the old path
deserves to be told where the work went and why — rather than finding nothing and
assuming a dead link.
