## The five things to get right before anything else

**1 · Guards are a knob axis on three detectors we already ship. They are not tube
variants.** `guard_sec` is a keyword on `coact_detect`
([`coact.py:79`](../../src/bugarach/detectors/coact.py#L79)), `loco_detect`
([`loco.py:333`](../../src/bugarach/detectors/loco.py#L333)) and
`rate_detect`/`rate_and_context` ([`rate.py:178`](../../src/bugarach/detectors/rate.py#L178)).
That is Tony's own 2026-08-25 ruling applied: CFAR and its variants are ways of setting
a bar over a signal the six detectors already compute, so they are **points in a
parameter space that exists**, not new rows in `DETECTORS`.

**A guarded tube is a separate, unstarted thing.** `build_tube`
([`nets.py:183`](../../src/bugarach/learn/nets.py#L183)) subtracts a learned surround
from a learned centre — **no offset and no hole**: there is no excised band and no
divide, so nothing in it is a `guard_sec` and nothing here transfers to it by analogy.
It is item **D** of the knob-axis file, scoped in
[`the four variants`](../todo/2026-08-23-four-variants-of-the-tube.md), and it is
deliberately listed there *"only so nobody builds the CFAR axis twice."*

⚠ **And the word is overloaded three ways in this tree.** `docs/handoffs/2026-08-27-the-guards-that-could-not-fail.md`
is about **checks that report success while looking at nothing** — a size canary, a board
gate, a liveness test. `tools/guard_branch.sh`, `tools/guard_local_board.sh`,
`tests/test_branch_guard.py` and `tests/test_local_board_guard.py` are that sense too.
None of them is a guard cell. A grep for `guard` returns all three populations.

`learn/nets.py`. Different objects at different stages. Read the glossary entry from
`1b82160` or later — before `#359` it still said *"bugarach has none; that absence is
the finding"*, which was written 2026-08-22 and true for exactly one day.

## What the code does today

| detector | knob | default | what the guard removes | who normalizes it |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **CoactDetect** | `guard_sec`, **`guard_norm`** | `0.0`, `"compact"` | a band of `guard_sec`, **centred on the test bin** — its window is centred, so a guard **holes** it | `"compact"` (shipped) lays the retained pieces end to end and shifts on the shorter line. `"exposure"` (added `6db9c7a`) removes the events and **keeps the window length** |
| **LoCo** | `guard_sec` | `0.0` | two bounds around the **anchor**, one per half-context | nothing. Its threshold pool is built over bins inside each half, so `exposure` is not a one-line change there and **no fix is offered** |
| **rate + context** | `guard_sec` | `0.0` | a band centred on each grid point, out of the **context** | itself — it recovers counts, subtracts, and divides by the retained span, so the context stays a *rate* |

Three implementation facts that are load-bearing and easy to lose:

- **CoactDetect's null is a circular shift _within_ the window.** That is why the guard
  cannot simply leave a hole: a shift defined on the original width would wrap events
  across the excised span and re-import exactly what the guard removed — *"a guard that
  looks like it works and does nothing"* (`a15f5e3`). Compaction is the avoidance; it is
  also the defect — see *the bar rises where the guard excised nothing*, below.
- **LoCo's guard excises around the ANCHOR, not the bin under test.** Thresholds are
  computed every `thr_step_sec` (15 s FAST, 30 s SLOW) and each bin inherits its nearest
  anchor's value, so a bin can sit up to 7.5 s from the band the guard actually touched.
  **The first run of `probe_guard_where_it_lands.py` scored occupancy at the bin and
  reported LoCo as showing nothing** (+0.0074 empty against +0.0098 occupied); scoring at
  the anchor turned the same data into **+0.0405 against −0.0093**. Do not rediscover this.
- **LoCo refuses a guard in `null_context_mode="symmetric"`**
  ([`loco.py:357`](../../src/bugarach/detectors/loco.py#L357)) and `rate` raises if the
  guard would consume the whole context. Both are deliberate.

## What is true right now

### The bar rises where the guard excised nothing, and the size of that rise is arithmetic

![Eight configurations of CoactDetect: under the shipped `compact` normalization the bar where the guard excised nothing rises to almost exactly the open circle marking C/(C−guard) — 1.09 at a 5 s guard, 1.51 at 20 s — while under `exposure` it sits on 1.00 and the bars where the guard did excise events fall much further, to 0.87 and 0.66](../learned/guard_exposure.png)

`nullmean` is a **density, not a count**: the chance a circularly-shifted event lands in
one bin width is `m / L`, so excising a band that held no events leaves every `m` alone
and takes `L` from `C` to `C − g`. The ratio is `C / (C − g)`, with no free parameter —
*"it would hold if the recording contained no biology at all."*

Measured, 4 seeds, `baseline_quiet`, 500 surrogates per bin
([`guard_prior_art_2026-08-26.md`](../reviews/guard_prior_art_2026-08-26.md)):

| recording | guard | norm | ratio where **empty** | predicted | ratio where **occupied** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| crowded | 20 s | `compact` (shipped) | **1.5092** ± 0.0070 | 1.5007 | 0.9923 — *looks like nothing happens* |
| crowded | 20 s | `exposure` | **1.0033** ± 0.0051 | 1.0000 | **0.6609** — *a third of the bar, in every seed* |

**So the sentence that made wide guards look self-defeating was measuring the
normalization, not the guard.** The occupied effect does not collapse at 20 s; it is the
largest in the table with a +50% factor sitting on top of it.

**LoCo has the same disease with a quantizer in front of it** — at a 5 s guard its halves
shorten 60 s → 57.5 s, factor **1.0437**, and it moves about **half** the prediction
(measured 1.0212) because its bar is a 99.9th percentile of an **integer-valued** pool
that a 4% density change often fails to move at all. No fix is offered for LoCo, and
inventing one is real work, not a keyword.

### In the crowded tail, one gap bin moves and the rest are zero

![Recall by each planted event's own nearest-neighbour gap. Top: the 20 s exposure guard tracks a no-guard control loosened to alpha 3e-3 in every bin except the tightest, where the guard sits about seven points higher. Bottom: guard minus control, paired over 24 seeds — the under-10-second bin is +0.071 with 17 of 24 seeds agreeing, and every wider bin sits on zero](../learned/guard_in_the_tail.png)

The tight bins start lower (recall 0.659 at `<10s` against 0.859 at `>60s`), so **any**
uniform loosening lifts them more in absolute terms — a tilt is what headroom looks
like. **So the control is the argument.** A no-guard run whose α is loosened until it
matches on *both* margins, picked from a sweep:

| | overall recall | precision |
|---|---|---|
| 20 s `exposure` guard | 0.871 | 0.909 |
| no guard @ α 3e-3 | 0.865 | 0.910 |

Guard minus control, paired, 24 seeds:

| gap bin | `<10s` | `10-20s` | `20-30s` | `30-60s` | `>60s` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| planted | 591 | 1220 | 783 | 1207 | 519 |
| delta | **+0.071** | −0.009 | −0.007 | +0.003 | −0.000 |
| sd across seeds | 0.073 | 0.031 | 0.035 | 0.007 | 0.019 |
| seeds agreeing | **17/24** | 9/24 | 7/24 | 4/24 | 1/24 |

The bin that moves is the only one where events sit inside each other's reference
window, and **headroom would tilt every bin in proportion to its remaining room** —
`10-20s` has nearly as much room and shows nothing.

**§4a's instrument was right and was defeated twice**: `CROWDED_RECORDING` has a 14 s
floor, so the bin carrying the signal did not exist in it, and `compact` was cancelling
most of what remained. `TAIL_RECORDING` drops the floor to 6 s — the seven real tail
recordings have minimum gaps of 6–26 s, median 8 — and **`interval_cv` was deliberately
not the knob**: it buys crowding by clumping, and reaching 0.5 that way needs a realized
CV of 1.2–1.6 while the real tail sits at 0.62–1.59, median 0.93. **The tail is dense
and Poisson-ish, not bursty.**

### And it still buys no detection you can measure

![F1 against alpha on the quiet, busy and crowded recordings, five guard configurations per panel. Every configuration's best point falls inside the shaded band, which is the no-guard best plus or minus its spread across 12 seeds. Moving alpha one decade moves F1 further than any guard does — on busy the curves span 0.27 to 0.63 across the grid](../learned/guard_norm_bench.png)

Best-F1 across the α grid the operating point is actually chosen with, 12 seeds: every
guard configuration on every recording lands **inside one seed sd of the no-guard entry
beside it**. Re-judged against the correct scale (SEM, 3.5× smaller) and re-run as a
paired per-seed test on the widest gap in the table, it still fails: **8 of 12 seeds,
paired *t* 1.85 on 11 df against 2.20.**

**Both are true and neither is the other's refutation.** A gain concentrated in ~14% of
events (591 of 4320 planted — the `<10s` bin) is worth about a point of overall recall — which is exactly what a best-F1
comparison cannot see.

## What is NOT shown — do not let these drop

- **The tail result is recall, not F1**, on **simulated** data fitted to real statistics,
  at a **20 s guard** — not the shipped `0.0` — and **nothing in it argues for turning a
  guard on anywhere.**
- **17 of 24 seeds, and the sd is the size of the effect.** Roughly five standard errors
  with every other bin flat, which is a **structural signature, not a p-value**. None is
  offered and 24 seeds could not support one.
- **`TAIL_RECORDING`'s aggregate crowding is 0.61, above the observed maximum of 0.57.**
  Deliberate: the count is raised to populate the tight bins, and **the bins are the unit
  of use, not the headline fraction.** It is a diagnostic and **nothing may be calibrated
  on it.**
- **The link from "the bar falls at occupied anchors" to any recall number is still
  unrun.** It was the largest gap in #310 and it is still the largest gap.
- **`exposure` may be the wrong repair even though `compact` is wrong.** Shifting on the
  full window lets retained events land inside the excised band — the null samples a
  stretch the observation was forbidden to use. Radar has no analogue, because its
  reference cells do not move. **A third option may be better than both.**
- **Two residuals nobody has explained.** The measurement sits ~0.5% *above* `C/(C−g)`
  when the splice argument predicts *below*, consistently at both widths on both
  recordings; and `exposure` empty is **1.005, not 1.000**, which on 8372 bins is several
  standard errors out. Both are flagged in the prior-art review's own *"where I think it
  is most likely wrong."*
- **"Occupied" is defined on the pooled event train.** A band with three events from one
  ROI counts the same as three from three ROIs, and only the second is coactivity.
- **Nothing here is about real slices.** One export folder was opened, once, to measure
  crowding — and detections are not ground truth, so a detector that misses crowded
  events **understates** crowding, which is the direction that flatters the bench.
- **The prior-art review is `Not murderboarded`, by its own first line.** Its citations
  carry a read-status table and most are **abstract-only**. Nothing from it goes outward
  without a murderboard and without Berge et al. §3–4 in hand.

## Traps, each of which has already cost someone

- **Do not tune the guard to make the crowded number look better.** It moves the number;
  that is the problem, not the solution.
- **Do not replace `maxlt`** (fork #8, `detector_history.md` §5.4 and §6.4). The
  combination rule is right for this preparation; **censoring inside it** is the
  prescribed fix.
- **Do not read a flat gain as success.** The control band exists to catch exactly that,
  and it caught it once already.
- **Do not compare at a fixed α.** `exposure` lowers a bar `compact` raised, so at a
  frozen α it buys recall and pays precision — which is what *any* threshold change does
  and is not evidence about a detector. Sweep the knob the operating point is chosen with.
- **Write the `--selftest` first.** The four probes that have one run guard 0 against
  guard 0 and demand every delta be **exactly** zero. Without it this whole subject is
  *"RNG drift with a mechanism attached"*, which is the failure §4a was corrected for
  twice.
- **A best-α picked by `argmax` over six values, scored on the same data, inflates both
  arms.** It did not matter in #329 because the effect failed anyway — but a paired test
  is sensitive enough that on a sweep where it *passed*, this would need holding out
  first.
- **Two of the three recordings in the guard sweep cannot show mutual masking at all.**
  `BENCH_RECORDING` under `baseline_quiet` and under `baseline_busy` plants events ≥120 s
  apart against a ±30 s reference window, so measured crowding is **0.00** on both and
  they test the **self-masking half alone**. Only the crowded row could ever show it —
  and `bench.py` says *"nothing should be calibrated on"* that one.
- **Report crowded-band gain minus control-band gain, never F1**, for anything in this
  family. A gain flat across nearest-neighbour gap is *"a threshold shift wearing a
  mechanism's clothes."*
