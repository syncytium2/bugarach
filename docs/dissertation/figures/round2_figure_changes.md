## What the chapter text will need (not changed here)

- **Figure 1.11 and §1.8.** "The resolved 0.2 s column" is now resolved on both records only for
  20 cells; the 10-cell point at 0.2 s is half-filled. The realistic-box counts are those at the
  tabulated event counts (2 of 40 fast, 16 of 40 slow, general form). They are not the
  `lab_values.json` counts the text quotes (10 and 19 of 40).
- **§1.4.6 "At realistic values".** The text's per-recording figures (1.06 [0.39, 1.53], 8 at the
  bound, 10 of 40 within; 17 and 19 of 40 slow) are from `lab_values.json`. Figure 1.Y C shows
  `realistic_counts.json`. Fast stream: 4 and 2 of 40 within (hard-core, general) for 7 cells, 8
  and 8 for 13 cells. Slow stream: 17 and 18, and 16 and 16.
- **Captions** for the two new figures are drafted in `README.md` as Figures 1.X and 1.Y.
