**Can a reader state what VIABLE, NARROWED and STOPPED require after one reading?** The broad outline, yes:
- **VIABLE:** both lab streams pass, and so does the Cossart leak test.
- **NARROWED:** one stream passes, or the Cossart leak test fails.
- **STOPPED:** both streams fail.

The edges, no. See the outcome-table finding below.

**Does a mechanism need a figure?**
- **Destruction test with planted twins: yes, required.** "Twins with the stream's own per-ROI counts and floor, with and without planted events, each pushed through rigid shift with the same key and scored by … selection-corrected coactivity excess" is five mechanisms in one sentence. A cold reader cannot rebuild what "retained share" is measured on.
- **Rigid shift: yes, a small schematic.** It would show three ROI rows, each slid by its own offset, with one onset falling off the window edge. Beside it would go circular shift (wraps around) and uniform dither (each onset moves on its own). "Nothing wrapped" and the count-loss gate only make sense against that contrast.
- **Where the figures go:** the page is frozen, so both belong in an amendment or at the head of the result write-up. The repo rule applies: no marks drawn on a raster, and cues go in a lane above it.

**Chart-type check (false friends):** does not apply, since the page has no figure.

**Tone:** headings use sentence case. PASS, VIABLE and the other capitalised labels are defined result labels, so they are acceptable. "SIGNED" in capitals and the heavy bolding are minor. Lists are formatted as lists.
