# Reviewer 2 (adversarial): findings on docs/proposals/2026-09-25-hebbian-coupling-detector.md and its Figure 1

I did not read docs/reviews/. The only scratch run I did was one simulation (inline `python3 -c`, nothing written into the repo). I also recomputed the figure's numbers by importing `measure()` from `tools/make_hebbian_kernel_figure.py`, without rendering.

**Bottom line.** The two stops are the whole safety case, and neither can fire as written:
- **The busy-core check (F1)** passed 9 of 9 pure-core recordings I simulated.
- **The just-STTC recovery arm (F2)** tunes the rule's knobs against the same objective it is then scored on, and has no margin.

Z is 0/0 for most pairs, including every silent ROI (F3). The squared-active-count control is a constant (F4). The page argues the sweep's lower bound (m ≥ 3) from a statistic the figure does not plot and that its own generator contradicts (F5). A penumbra-subtracted export folder that the page says does not exist is registered in the repo's own dataset config (F10).
