# Methods review: slow co-modulation page, its figures and code (commit ab736cd)

The most serious problem is the count-variance ratio after CoactDetect's episodes are removed. It is divided by the null of the unremoved recording, which has more onsets. The removal rows in the table, Figure 1's hatched bars and the lab slow-stream story all rest on that ratio, and all of them need redoing. The correlogram code itself is correct.

**Where the new numbers come from.** I reran the measurement against the lab export and the Dard et al. folder. First I reproduced the run's circular-shift variances exactly (largest relative difference 0.0). Then I added two things: the circular null of the trains after removal, and the variance left after fitting a straight line to each recording's counts. The scripts and outputs are `<scratchpad>/review2/r6/denom_check.py`, `null_calib.py` and their `.json` files.

## Checked, no finding
- **Chance level:** (N² − Σn²)(L−k)/L² counts ordered pairs of distinct ROIs correctly, and is unbiased under circular shift.
- **Zero lag:** halved in both the observed and expected counts, so it is consistent. The brute-force test covers it.
- **Lag bins:** the lab frame interval is 0.1 s throughout. Dard et al. runs 0.0926–0.119 s, so the first bin holds 3 or 4 frame lags, which does not matter.
- **Variance ratio mechanics:** ddof cancels in the ratio. The circular null is a mean over 8 draws, and I reproduced it exactly. Only full bins are used. The 2-minute blocks line up with the 1-minute bins.
- **Rigid shift:** `rigid_frames` is applied before trimming, so onsets can move into the trimmed window, as intended.
- **CoactDetect removal:** onsets are matched to episodes with the same half-open bins CoactDetect uses, and merged gaps are included. The fast and slow operating points match `bench.OPERATING_POINTS` and the docstring.
- **Dard et al. windows:** the window starts at the first onset (lead-in median 17 s, maximum 72 s) and ends one frame past the last onset. Both ends fall inside the 20 s trim.
- **Synthetic worlds:** built exactly as `fair_bakeoff._make_recording` builds them. The OU multiplier is stationary and averages to 1.
