3. **Figure 3C (README 122, 229-231; script 394-395)** · Panel C's "planted share" comes from `mcr.truth` (`measure_coordination_rates.py:393-398`). That function counts **distractors (decoys) as planted coordinated events**: `ks` is taken over `gt.events + gt.distractors`. So in 3C the right answer includes 6 decoys × 6 cells per recording, while panels A and B have no decoys. The README says only "the decoys on". A reader will assume decoys are negatives, which is what the bench docstring calls them. The truth definitions also differ slightly. A and B count planted onsets after per-cell frame dedupe, as a share of all onsets (script 160-165). `mcr.truth` counts `ks.sum()` before dedupe, in Hz, divided here by the post-dedupe rate. The second difference is negligible at these rates. · Low-Medium · Add one clause to the 3C caption and to README 229-231: "the tool's check counts the decoys as coordinated, so they are part of the right answer here." · yes
