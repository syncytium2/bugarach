**What holds (checked, no finding):**
- **Window sharing:** g₂'s closed form and g₃'s table match Monte Carlo to within 4·10⁻⁴.
- **Expected excess counts:** the hard-core f₂ and f₃ follow from factorial cumulants, with a per-cell Bernoulli surrogate giving the (1−1/N) and (1−1/N²) factors. `solve()`'s fixed-model mapping, k = f₃/f₂ + 2, matches.
- **Frame rounding:** with windows on the frame grid, rounding only shifts the bin edges by half a frame, so it changes nothing, exactly and not just in expectation.
- **Chance clusters:** 60q(1−q)² matches `_clusters` (merge_bins = 2), and the gather never removes a group at these defaults.
- **Order closed forms:** τ = (2/π) arcsin ρ leads to (2/π) arctan r. The variance identity behind the M-template form is correct.
- **Membership:** I re-derived 1 + (E−1)/M as an exact ratio of expected variances.
- **Kish:** the form of deft² checks out against pp. 63–64.
- **No-order mean:** the exact lattice convolution in `kendall_w` is sound.
- **Citations checked:** the Louis 2010 claims (binomial capture, slope change at 2s+1, overlapping windows ignored, Fig. 6), minimum contrast on the K-function in Waagepetersen, Mariani's 9.7 s against 861.8 s, and Kreuz's eq. 14 giving the factor (k−1)/(N−1).
- **Shipped parameters:** the validations ran at them. Aim 1a used the tool's windows (1, 2, 4 s) and 20 surrogates, and the assessor's 1 s bins, merge rule and ±1.5 s gather. Its 120 surrogates against a shipped 1000 affect only outputs that were not used. The separation runs used count bin 2 s with a 3 s merge gap, and the assessor at 1 s, merge 2, gather 1.5.
