# Role 2, "DOI or Die": citation and reference validation of Chapter 1 (a3d06b04…)

**Committee verdict: I would not sign this chapter yet.** One blocking item: the chapter says every source was obtained and read, and that is false. Six major items:
- one attribution credits the wrong paper and hides that the work came from the same group;
- one citation has no reference entry;
- one method's origin is traced to a later user rather than its source;
- one precedent is mischaracterized;
- several fields that hold direct analogs of the "not found" claims were never searched;
- the people who could settle the novelty claims have not been asked.

## How I checked
- **Library holdings:** I checked every reference against `01-lit/` by filename, both the shelf and the whole library.
- **Bibliographic metadata:** I checked metadata for all 31 entries against Crossref, by DOI or bibliographic query. The Kreuz 2022 and 2026 papers were included.
- **Quoted claims:** I read each quoted claim against the text extractions: Grün 1999, Louis 2010, Pazienti 2008, Kruskal 1958, Kreuz 2017, Mariani 2025, Kreuz 2022 (XML), Cecchini 2021, Waagepetersen 2007, Diggle & Gratton 1984, Thomas 1949, Neyman & Scott 1952, Durbin 1951, Skillings & Mack 1981, Coppersmith 2006, Shah & Wainwright, CuBIC (XML), Wang 2024 and Williams 2020. For Kish 1995 I rendered pp. 63–64 from the scan and read them.
- **Prior art:** I ran my own searches beyond the chapter's `prior_art/` and `kreuz_sort/` records, and read those records to see what they found and what the chapter left out.
