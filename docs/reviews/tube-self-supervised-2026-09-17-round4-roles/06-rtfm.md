## Checked and correct

- **Rigid shift, two implementations.** `rigid_frames` (numpy) and `bugarach.surrogates.rigid_shift` (Elephant `dither_spike_train`, `edges=True`) both compute floor(k + 0.5 + u) and drop anything outside [0, L). I ran both on 4,000 identical trains with onsets at the edges. Offsets matched (SD 57.4 vs 57.6 frames; the ±J end bins carry half weight in both), the shift was exact per ROI, and the keep probability matched at every position. The shipped test is weaker than this, but the construction is the same, edges included.
- **Elephant 1.2.1 and the surrogate literature.** Elephant drops at the ends (`edges=False` clips) and `trial_shifting` wraps within each trial. Louis, Borgelt & Grün 2010 (ch. 17) roll the train, with the equal start/end rate reason. Stella 2022: TR-SHIFT is per trial and per neuron, D = 25 ms, called "the most robust". Harrison & Geman call Pipa 2007 "closely related". Pipa 2008 shifts whole trains.
- **Training crops.** A margin of ceil(J) + 1 frames keeps dropped onsets out of every crop. Softplus equals ln 2 at chance. Top 1 % of 4,096 frames is 40 frames. The networks have no dropout or batch-norm.
- **Leak tests.** The per-ROI test is a paired, no-intercept logistic forced choice with mouse-grouped folds. The bootstrap resamples mice, keeps duplicate mice in one fold, and refits: a proper classifier two-sample test. Edge-band features are masked. On the fast stream the rigid-shift upper bounds (0.518–0.534) exclude the module's preset 55 % effect. The run has 1,501 pairs, 44 mice and 84 recordings. The hand-built bank's widening (±1 frame), centre widths (1, 2, 4, 8 frames) and surround ratio (8) match `tube` at initialization.
- **Nadeau–Bengio.** Variance × (1/4 + 2/6), t on 3 df. My recomputation of line − tube gives +0.009 to +0.078 and p = 0.027, and the formula matches the corrected resampled t.
- **Thresholds and scoring.** The label-free scan runs downward, takes the maximum over three shifts, and flags the grid floor. Supervised and untrained thresholds on the bench use J = 10 s. The truth-reading threshold uses `pick_threshold` on 2 validation recordings, pooled through `pool_scores`. Matching is greedy and one-to-one, so `tiny`'s 0.125 is exactly recall 2/30 at precision 1.0.
- **Constants.** Count baselines: ±2 frames = ±0.2 s, 301 frames ≈ 30 s, 101 frames ≈ 10 s, at 0.1 s per frame. The head's receptive field is 127 samples (±6.3 s). Participation uses ±2 frames. Activity-matched chance uses each detector's own widths. Supervised and `count_excess` real-recording numbers match the run record. The twin check has 60 twin-draws per scorer and 120 checkpoints.
