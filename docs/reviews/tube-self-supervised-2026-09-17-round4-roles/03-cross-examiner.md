# Cross-Examiner review: `<worktree>/docs/learned/tube_self_supervised/README.md` (commit f2278da)

Two claims in the page are wrong against the tools and `summary.json`:

- **The rigid-shift displacement on real recordings.** The page says the supervised models' and count baselines' label-free thresholds on real recordings are set at *J* = 20 s. The tool and `summary.json` both say 10 s.
- **"No more co-activity than chance."** The trained models' share of events holding three or more ROIs does not sit inside the chance range. In 7 of the 10 conditions it is above that condition's own chance rate. The claim appears in a finding bullet, in Figure 5's lead sentence, in the notes under Figure 5, and in decision 3 of *What waits on Tony*.

Everything else is medium or low. The page also contradicts itself in several places, and three companion documents still carry claims the page has replaced.
