**Mode.** Single-pass self-review walking every role's checklist in turn, not a
parallel subagent fan-out. The deliverable is one page — two figures and eight
short paragraphs — and this session is configured not to spawn agents unasked.
The process permits scaling *how* the roles run; it does not permit dropping
any, and none was dropped. Role 10 ran in full against every render.

**Why this page was reviewed at all.** It is a public portfolio surface that now
publishes a real recording, and the prose makes claims about detector behaviour.
CLAUDE.md routes document deliverables here before they ship.

## Findings and adjudication

**F1 · role 6 (RTFM) + role 1 · confirmed factual error · FIXED.**
The lead caption said *"these detectors all count distinct ROIs coactive."*
`GLOSSARY.md` defines **rate+context (RateDetect)** as a *population-rate excess
vs a slow context rate* and **SPIKE-synch** as a *tau-capped adaptive
SPIKE-synchronization profile* — neither counts distinct ROIs. The claim is
false for two of the six. Replaced with what is true of all six: they find
moments that stand out from the rest of the recording. **`docs/generator.md`
carries the same overstatement** ("every one of them counts distinct ROIs
coactive") and is on another branch — filed as a todo rather than edited here.

**F2 · role 1 · FIXED.** "matched … to 1e-9" now reads "to 1e-9 on committed
fixtures", which is what FOUNDATIONS §2 actually claims.

**F3 · role 1 · FIXED.** "reproduces this page from a clean clone" was untrue
without playwright chromium. The load-bearing claim is about data, not build
environment, so it now reads: the build opens no data store and generates every
other figure from a seed.

**F4 · role 4 · FIXED.** "every number that could be matched was" is an absolute
that the tree does not support — interval CV, minimum separation and the
background *shape* are not matched. Replaced with the enumerated list the figure
itself states: ROI count, duration, per-ROI rate, participation, jitter.

**F5 · role 4 · FIXED.** "That is why …" asserted causation from a single
real/generated pair and one detector. Now scoped: "on the pair above".

**F6 · role 10 · regression introduced by this change · FIXED.** The new
`--hero` render strips the legend, which is where the diagnostic explains its
glyphs — so ✗ (false alarm), ○ (duplicate call) and the planted row's ▲/▼
appeared on a public page with nothing identifying them. The caption now carries
that key. *This defect did not exist before the change that caused it; it is the
"fix creates a defect" class the process warns about.*

**F7 · role 8 · blocking · FIXED.** "ROI" was used throughout and never defined;
the six detectors were counted but never named; the lanes read `CIC`, `coact`,
`rate`, `sync` with no expansion anywhere. Tony flagged this abbreviation
directly on 2026-08-15 — *"CIC is not CICADA to anyone who has met the other
CIC."* The page now defines ROI on first use, names all six, and maps every
short label in the caption.

**F8 · role 11 · structural · FIXED.** The spine ran: mismatch → parity → posture
→ known answers → diagnostic → *why any of this matters*. The motivation — two
weeks lost to a benchmark that did not match reality — arrived **last**, so the
reader saw the problem's evidence before learning there was a problem. Moved the
cost section ahead of the payoff figure. The lead figure stays first, per Tony's
instruction; the arc is now problem → cost → what the generator carries → the
method working on known answers.

**F9 · role 5 · FIXED.** "marginal numbers" is statistics jargon on a page
written for outside readers; now "the rate, the jitter and the participation".

**F10 · role 5 · found by blind round 1 · FIXED.** The lead caption's bold
opener was *"A real recording, and the generator asked to imitate it"* — the
figure's own baked-in title, word for word. Rewritten to say what the image does
not.

**F11 · role 10 · found by blind round 1 · FIXED AT SOURCE.** The published
figure's lower y-axis read **`9.5 mHz/RC`**. The source string is `mHz/ROI`: the
bottom panel spends part of its 250 px on an x-axis the top panel does not have,
so its rotated label was silently clipped. Shipping that on the lead figure of a
portfolio page is exactly the clipping class role 10 exists for. Fixed in
`make_reality_check.py` by shortening the label (the ROI count is already in the
header line) and **regenerating from the real store** — not cropped around.
Verified the regeneration preserved every number the page quotes: same slice,
37 ROI, 30 min, 9.5 mHz, LoCo 5 vs 10, 12 planted.

**F12 · role 8 · found by blind round 2 · FIXED AT SOURCE.** The figure header
named `simulate_coordination` — an internal code identifier, in text now facing
the public. Replaced with "the generator" and regenerated.

**F13 · role 3 · FIXED.** "many neurons fire together" — the detectors read ROI
onset streams, not neurons. Now "one cell's worth of signal", with ROI defined.
