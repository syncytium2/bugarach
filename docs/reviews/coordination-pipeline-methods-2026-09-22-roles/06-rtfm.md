## Also checked, and correct as written
- The exact catch probability Σ min(w, gap)/L is correct (the measure of a union of arcs). Mean and variance are Σp and Σp(1−p). LoCo's quantile is the exact Poisson-binomial quantile.
- Background: per-cell rate is Gamma with shape 0.275 and mean equal to the background rate. Multipliers of mean 1, drawn per cell, have shape 1.547 on 300 s bins and 1.388 on 60 s bins. Counts are Poisson per bin, placed uniformly, rounded to 0.1 s.
- Participation: 10, 6 and 3 of 33 cells. Distractors: 6 cells each, uniform in 120–1,100 s. Elevated-rate block: 0.06 events s⁻¹ with a 30 s linear ramp, and the planted-event exclusion is padded by 120 s. Widths come from `MEASURED_WIDTH_QUANTILES`, drawn independently of whether an event was planted.
- Matching: one-to-one, closest pair first, 2.5 s gap to the call interval, ties broken by distance to the call's midpoint. Precision excludes the elevated-rate block (`BenchResult.precision`). Table 2's limits equal `bench.py` MAX_PROBE_PER_MIN, MAX_FALSE_POSITIVES_PER_HOUR, MAX_PRECISION_DROP and MAX_CROWDED_DROP.
- Training: `BCEWithLogitsLoss` with pos_weight = (1−p)/p pooled over training recordings, and Adam. Crops are 4,096 frames (409.6 s), half centred on a positive frame. The threshold grid has exactly 41 values from 1e-4 to 0.9999. Frames at or above the threshold form calls, with a 20-frame (2 s) merge. Labels run from first to last participant onset, with distractors and the block labelled 0.
- SPIKE-synch: τ is the minimum of the four half inter-spike intervals, capped at τ_max, with a strict `<` (Kreuz et al. 2015).
- The nested cross-validation matches the run's `meta.json`: 48 seeds in 4 folds of 12, 72 tuning recordings, 24 configurations, 3 training seeds for tuning and 5 for refits, 10 fitting recordings per refit, and a 1.6× budget against CoactDetect at its Table 1 setting.
- Width and amplitude measurement: 0.5 s gap, ±1 s aperture, core chosen by most distinct cells, amplitude = cells / max(width, one frame), undefined for a single cell.
