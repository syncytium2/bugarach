**Bottom line.**
- The metadata of all three listed references is correct; each one resolves on Crossref.
- Two of the attributions misstate what the cited paper says:
  - **Binned SCE.** Cossart 2003 did not use a circular-shift null, a 98th percentile or 10 s bins.

- The answer to the caller's question: the capped window **does not belong to Satuvuori et al. 2017**. That paper's "adaptive" variant (A-SPIKE-synchronization, with a minimum relevant time scale, MRTS) is a different mechanism, and it is off in this lineage.
- The section cites nothing for CICADA, even though locust is derived from its code.
- The PySpike verification claim is too strong: parity with PySpike holds only without the cap.
