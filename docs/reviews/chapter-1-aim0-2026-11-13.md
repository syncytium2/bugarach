# Murderboard run: Chapter 1 of the dissertation (Aim 0), revised opening

**The problem.** Tony read the October chapter and asked for a different opening. In his words,
it should lead with a figure of coordinated events on a noisy background, walk a reader through
three detectors (counting in fixed bins, counting in a sliding window, and a basic CFAR, a
threshold set from the local background), show where each succeeds and fails in a raster built for
the purpose, and only then introduce the tools that characterize coordination. Those tools are
the cross-cell correlogram and its half width at half maximum, shown on a sharp set and a
jittered set, with the mathematics behind them. He also asked for "toy" to be dropped and every
term to be defined at first use. The chapter was rewritten to that outline; the new opening and its
figures are 1.1–1.3 of [`docs/dissertation/chapter-1-aim0.md`](../dissertation/chapter-1-aim0.md),
drawn by [`figures/ch1_intro_figures.py`](../dissertation/figures/ch1_intro_figures.py). The rest of
the chapter moved down one section number and was revised to match. The murderboard was
re-vendored first (PR #935), then run for three rounds of 11 roles each, followed by a follow-up
pass.

**What was found, and what changed.** The reviews caught errors of substance, not only of prose:

- the story told for the third detector situation was wrong (it was a failure of the merge rule,
  not of the threshold);
- two planted events sat on bin borders, so fixed bins were tested on luck; they were moved;
- CFAR's masking by a nearby event was asserted but not shown; a control now shows it (CFAR's
  detection of event 2 goes from 0.45 to 0.98 with event 4 left out);
- the plug-in threshold was presented as Finn & Johnson's CFAR, which it is not: theirs scales
  the threshold for the size of the reference sample, and this one does not;
- the realistic values had no stated provenance; they now carry their source, their intervals,
  the fact that they pool the four groups, and the fact that a mouse contributes up to three
  slices;
- "readable" was used without a definition (it now means at least 35 of 40 recordings within
  0.8–1.25 of planted), and the 1.5.7 summary contradicted Table 1.5 until the follow-up pass
  caught it;
- the conclusion read as a statement about real segments; it now says it holds in simulation at
  the lab's values;
- references were corrected: Finn 1967 was opened and is cited by page, Finn 1966 was added,
  and Sheppard 1899 and Grün 1996 are marked as not held.

**What is not settled.** The run stopped at the cap (three rounds) without converging. The open
majors are structural and figure-level, and they need Tony's call rather than another edit:

- 1.3 gives the predictions before 1.4 poses the question it answers;
- the long theory sections have no figure, and there is no "events needed" figure for the
  headline;
- sixteen older figures are not redrawn for print, and their panel titles still say "record"
  where the text says "rerun";
- the results are pooled across groups, although per-group values exist; quoting them is a group
  finding, which is `fireflies`' by default (ADR-0017).

See *Residual ⚠* below.
