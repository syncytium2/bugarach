## Summary for the committee

Overall, the analysis code reuses production code well. Wherever a number depends on the tools, the scripts call `measure_coordination_rates.solve`, `measure`, `_frames` and `truth`. They also call `assess._coact_count`/`_clusters`/`assess_coactivity`, `detectors.count.count_detect`, `event_floor.floor_of`, `simulate._quantize`/`_place_renewal`, `bugarach.assembly` and `measure_jitter_correlogram.pairs`/`hwhm`/`_sim`/`pooled`. The assessor parameters are read from `assess_coactivity`'s signature, and the surrogate spacing from `mcr.SURR_SPACING`.

Where code was copied, the copy is usually checked against the original:
- `assessor_jitter.clusters_detail` asserts that its SDs equal `_clusters` cluster by cluster, and checks `jit_obs` against `assess_coactivity`.
- `split.py` checks its merge automaton against `_clusters`.

I checked three more copies myself:
- **`event_train.py` against `separation/real_intervals/intervals.py`.** The port reproduces every coefficient (a2, b2, a3, b3, c3) to 5 decimals for hard-core, Poisson and fast-stream renewal trains, at w = 1 and 4 s and σ = 0.105 and 0.5 s.
- **`theory.load_gaps` against `bugarach.real_intervals.load_gaps`.** Both give the same 174 fast and 440 slow gaps (`gaps_for_generator.json`; means 100.58 and 51.35 s).
- **`correlogram_vs_joint.py`'s inline calibration against `measure_jitter_correlogram.main()`.** It matches: both regimes, seeds 1–24, monotone check, NaN off the curve.

I would sign off after the four major items below are fixed. None is blocking: in each, the chapter either misstates or leaves unused something the production code already does. None invalidates a reported number.
