## What my method CANNOT see

Stated so the clean results above are not read as broader than they are:

- **Inside-SVG occlusion.** My overlap test used DOM bounding boxes for block elements plus eyes on zoomed crops. I did not pixel-diff for two SVG elements that visually touch while their boxes do not overlap (or overlap harmlessly). Fine-grained tick-label-on-tick-label collisions inside a plot could survive this.
- **Font substitution.** I rendered with chromium's resolution of the page's font stack on this machine. A reader whose machine falls back to a different face could get wider labels and collisions I would not have seen.
- **Print / PDF pagination.** I never rendered to a paper size, so figures or tables split across a page break are unchecked.
- **Scroll-to-reveal not actually exercised.** For finding #1 I confirmed `overflow-x:auto` and the 211 px overflow, which means the content is reachable in principle; I did not programmatically scroll and re-screenshot to confirm the `outcome` column becomes fully legible once scrolled.
- **External link targets.** The two relative links (`../tuned_vs_coact/...`) were checked for shape only, not resolved against a built site.
- **Sub-2px overlaps** were filtered out of the pairwise test as noise.
