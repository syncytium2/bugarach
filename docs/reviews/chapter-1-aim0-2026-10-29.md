# Murderboard run — Chapter 1 of the dissertation (Aim 0)

**The problem.** Tony asked for Aim 0, the theory that predicts what the coordination-rates tool,
the assessor and the order and membership statistics return for one simulated source, to be
written as a dissertation's first chapter. He then asked for a revision round run "like a
dissertation committee meeting", followed by the full murderboard. There is no committee; the
dissertation is an exercise in how far Tony and Claude can take a piece of research together. The
chapter is [`docs/dissertation/chapter-1-aim0.md`](../dissertation/chapter-1-aim0.md), with its
figures in [`docs/dissertation/figures/`](../dissertation/figures/README.md) and the follow-up
analyses the reviewers asked for in
[`ch1_followups/`](../learned/synthetic_validation/ch1_followups/README.md). This run reviewed it
in one review round and two blind verify rounds, 11 roles each, with a follow-up pass after each.

**Stopping reason:** the round cap. Round 3 still carried two blocking findings and majors, so a
follow-up pass fixed them without a further blind round:
- **Realistic window (role 4, blocking; roles 1, 3, 5 and 8, major).** The recording window is
  now defined from `windows.csv` (about 20 min; fast median 1 event, slow 2; 28 and 25 of 66
  windows hold none). The joint fit and the tool were rerun on 20-minute windows at 1, 2, 4, 8 and
  16 events (`window_counts.py`, Table 1.4). The chapter's verdict is now: participants readable
  in one window for 13-cell events from two events, 7-cell events only from about eight; jitter
  never.
- **Mid-review figure edits (role 10, blocking).** This was a process error by the author: the
  figures helper was started on round-3 fixes while the reviewers were still running, and it
  changed Figure 1.6 (then numbered 1.6, now 1.5) and the script behind Figure 1.1 in the working
  tree. The committed tree the other ten roles read was unchanged. The figures were finished and
  regenerated after all eleven reports were in, and the captions now match them.
- **The assessor's own null (roles 6 and 7).** `jit_null` and `jit_excess` were measured at the
  shipped 1000 surrogates (`assessor_null.py`): the null equals the chance-cluster spread and the
  excess stays flat, so the tool's own correction does not rescue the reading. The chapter says so,
  and names the step that plants the raw reading into simulator parameters.
- **The surrogate (roles 6 and 7).** Described as the tool builds it (distinct offsets two windows
  apart); Figure 1.3 A redrawn with the tool's own function; the hard-core form now says it assumes
  independent offsets, and the size of the dependence is given.
- **Statistics (roles 1, 3, 4 and 6).** Dependence-aware p-values for the condition-window counts
  (`dependence_null.py`); the shipped pooled reporting path checked (`pooled_path.py`); the
  slow-stream miss restated as one coupled deviation of up to 3.3 SE; 2.4 → 2.2; 3.7 → 3.5.
- **Thresholds and background (roles 4 and 8).** K = 4 at 0.0096 Hz shown to sit on the budget
  (it crosses at 0.00964 Hz) and 5 recommended; chance clusters on the generator's bursty and
  unequal-rate backgrounds measured (`chance_bursty.py`).
- **Order and membership (roles 4, 6 and 7).** The exact no-order mean qualified to untied onsets,
  and a tie-corrected mean added; rounding rerun at the cells the chapter uses
  (`order_rounding_cells.py`); the ranking renamed a Borda score; membership detection reported
  conditional on a repeat, with the test's two statistics (`membership_repeat.py`).
- **Structure and figures (roles 5, 8, 9 and 11).** The two-source claim derived in 1.4.3; the
  fourth limit (1.6) given its job in 1.1; Figure 1.9 moved after the joint fit; a realistic-values
  subsection (1.4.7); new figures for 1.4.5 and 1.5.2; "hard-core form" used throughout; the
  conclusion rewritten around what a realistic window supports; the rederivation moved to *About
  this chapter*; symbols renamed (τ_min, ν_c, ν_e, e_a).

The follow-up pass after round 3 was checked only by its author; its fixes were not re-verified by
a blind round.

**Calibration.** This review found and fixed the defects in the reports listed above (about 39
rated blocking and 150 major across the three rounds, by the rough count in the table). It is not a
correctness proof. The convergence table measures how quickly reviewers stopped finding things, not
whether anything remains. Every reviewer was a Claude agent on the same model as the author, and no
human has reviewed the derivations.
