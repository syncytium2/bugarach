## Checked and found correct

- **Chance floor:** `chance_floor` gives 6 at 0.0096 and 0.01 Hz and 12 at 0.05 Hz. This matches `event_floor.py` (FA_PER_HOUR = 1, 2 s sliding window, a call is one run) and the repository's simulated floor (`separation/assessor_split/derivation.md` l.54: 6 and 12). The binomial/up-crossing algebra is right; `binom.sf(K−1)` = P(X ≥ K).
- **`sliding_count`:** the window is (t−w, t] (numpy `searchsorted` semantics checked against its docs).
- **`local_rate`:** guard and reference windows as stated: 30 s each side, 5 s guard.
- **Masking explanation for event 2:** event 1 sits in the left reference window and 3a in the right. Finn & Johnson do name masking by a second target (p. 1, pp. 34–35). Rohling 1983 describes guard cells as routine (pp. 3, 13) and OS-CFAR resisting a second target (p. 13). Perkel, Gerstein & Moore use "cross-correlation histogram" with a rate-based expected level (pp. 7, 17).
- **Predicted lag distribution (Fig. 1.3):**
  - The triangular rounding kernel is valid because the simulator's event times are continuous (`_place_renewal`, not on the grid).
  - k is fixed at `matlab_round(10/33·33)` = 10, so k(k−1) = 90 is exact.
  - `quad(points=[L])` is used on finite limits, as the scipy docs require.
- **Correlogram arithmetic:** `np.roll(pop, -lag)` pairs (i at t, j at t+lag), and the circular expected count is consistent with it.
- **HWHM:** 2√(ln 2)·σ is correct. g₂ = ½ at w = 2.079σ is confirmed numerically.
- **`excess_cumulants`:** called correctly (frame integers, L, wf, N_SURR, [0] = f₂). The normalisation λwk(k−1) matches theory's f₂ = p·k(k−1)·g₂ − (pk)²(1 − 1/N). The "within 0.02" claim holds at every window.
- **Fig. 1.3 and §1.3.4 numbers** match the log: 18.2/18.5, 0.176/0.174, 4.6/4.8, 0.796/0.835 (4.7 % narrower), 1.47e-2, 4.41e-3, 2.07e-8, 0.017/0.019.
