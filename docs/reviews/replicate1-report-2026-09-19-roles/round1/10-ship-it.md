## Suggested fixes, in priority order

1. **Phone-width figure text (High).** At narrow widths, give each SVG a minimum rendered width inside a scroll wrapper, or use a narrow-layout variant. Alternatively, raise the viewBox font sizes so that 358 px placed yields ≥ 9 px: roughly 27 px in a 1000-wide viewBox. Do the same for the Figure 1 PNG, for example with a tall, cropped variant for narrow widths.
2. **Table 1 at 390 (High).** Split it into two stacked tables (untuned plus F1 alone; under the budget), or turn it so runs are rows. At minimum, keep each key dot on the same line as its run name (`white-space:nowrap`), and give the table a visible cue that it scrolls sideways.
3. **Figure 1 marks (Medium).** Add to the caption a red ×, drawn in its colour, meaning "call near no planted event (false alarm)", and say that a bar's width is the detection's window. Name the x-axis "time (minutes)" or define *t*.
4. **Figure 8 failed seeds (Medium).** Give the F1 = 0 seeds a visible hatched or outlined placeholder box of fixed height, or a ▼ glyph labelled "called nothing", so both failed pairs share one glyph.
5. **Figure 6 mean ticks (Medium).** Draw the mean ticks after the dots, taller and edge-outlined, and widen the gap between panels (at least 40 px) so "0.8" and "0.2" separate.
6. **Panel letters (Medium).** Letter the panels of Figures 1, 3 and 6 (A/B/C), and rewrite the "top / bottom / left / middle / right" captions in Figures 1, 3, 4, 6 and 7 to use the letters.
7. **Box colours (Medium).** Identify every box fill in its own caption: peach in Figures 3 and 4, green in Figure 4, blue versus white in Figure 2. Better still, stop reusing one fill for different concepts across figures. Raise the dark-mode contrast between the `--held` and `--box` fills.
8. **Low items:** the duplicate SVG ids, `<title>` and meta tags, hidden coincident dots in Figure 7, "second − first" naming the runs, the tooltip vocabulary, blank cells in Table 1, the Figure 8 "refit seed" placement, the 16% white margin inside the Figure 1 PNG, and the Figure 5 key colour versus the tinted squares.
