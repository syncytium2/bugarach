## The arc I judged it against

Default analysis arc, doubled because the report legitimately carries two questions: **the problem → what it costs → the method → what the method gets wrong → the fix → the evidence → the residual risk → the decision.** The report *states* its own deviation ("Two questions follow, and the night answered them separately") — a stated deviation, which is allowed and to its credit. I judged the body against that stated map. Its delivered order is question 1 → question 3 → question 2 → question 2-on-real-data, which is not the map it published, and that mismatch is the root of several findings below.

Two things the order gets right and should survive editing: **caveats travel with their claim** inside Figure 1 (the "⚠ The wave is not a tilt" paragraph sits immediately under the wave number it qualifies — that is the correct pattern), and **the decisions come after the limits that bound them**, which is the right way round.
