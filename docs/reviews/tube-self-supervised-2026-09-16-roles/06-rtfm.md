## What checked out

Worth recording, because these were the specific things the task asked me to doubt:

- **conv1d shapes, padding and axis are correct.** `x.reshape(b*n,1,t)` → `vote.reshape(b,n,-1,t)` preserves the (batch, ROI) ordering; kernels are `2k+1` with `padding=k`, giving exact 'same' length; the Gaussians are symmetric so torch's cross-correlation-not-convolution makes no difference. **No filter runs along the wrong axis.**
- **The smear really is peak-normalised** (measured kernel peaks `[1,1,1,1]`) and **the sigmoid bound really is applied per ROI before pooling.** One ROI with 1 onset votes 0.982; the same ROI with 8 onsets votes 1.000 — bounded, as claimed, though bursting still buys 1.8 %. The DoG is exactly zero-sum, so a flat count cancels.
- **The label-free rule is legitimate across models with different score scales** — this was the sharpest question in the brief and the answer is yes. The grid is built from quantiles of *that model's own* surrogate logits, so multiplying a model's logits by any *c* > 0 rescales the grid identically and leaves the event counts and the chosen operating point unchanged. Scanning **down** and breaking at the first violation is also the right defence against the merged-detection trap, since event count is not monotone in threshold. The one gap: if no candidate ever violates, it returns `grid[-1]` (the surrogate median) with no warning, where `pick_threshold` would raise `EdgeOfRange`.
- **The supervised arm's logit conversion is right** — `pick_threshold` returns a probability, `probs()` returns logits, and `np.log(p/(1-p))` is applied before comparing. Easy to get wrong; it isn't.
- **Train/threshold/score separation holds** at the fold boundary: `fold_maker(sim_recording, split().train(held))` makes the held-out fold unreachable by construction, `oracle_threshold` reuses exactly `pick_threshold`'s val seeds, and `pin_threads()` is called in both `train` and `ssl_train`. The defect in finding 10 is *within* the val block, not across it.
- **The probe's plants are paired and deterministic** — I reproduced all twelve field/plant scores exactly — and the ROI set is identical across kinds at a given K, which is what makes the ratio the right statistic.
- **Every cross-checkable number in the prose matches its artifact**: the Panel A table against `bakeoff.json`; the probe ratios (1.83/1.99/1.32, 1.82/1.75/1.14, 1.34/1.43/1.17) against `line_vs_fuzz.json`; the agreement shares (76–77 %, 40–44 %, 47–72 %, 19–30 %) and their chance rates against `summary.json`; the 72-parameter delta against the architecture (3 extra channels × 8 × 3). **The arithmetic is sound throughout** — the findings above are about which numbers were chosen and what they were said to mean, not about miscalculation.
