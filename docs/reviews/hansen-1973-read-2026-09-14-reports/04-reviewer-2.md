**Adversarial review (Reviewer 2): Hansen 1973 update to `docs/detector_history.md` §4.1, `docs/GLOSSARY.md` and the proposal footer**

I checked the central claim against the page images, not just the OCR. I rendered pp. 325–332 and the figure captions from the loan PDF. It is a 12-page scan with a numeric filename in the local Downloads folder, not on the shelf. What I found: none of the greatest-of passages (p. 326 bottom, p. 327 top, the Fig. 5/6/7 captions on p. 331) carries a citation. The paper never calls the technique new. The garbled rule on p. 327 reads "N taken as the total number of reference noise observations divided by √2". The OCR drops the √.

So "neither cites a source for it nor presents it as new" is **supported**, and "seven years" (1973 to 1980) and "does not say it is proposing the technique" are literally true. The problems are in what the text concludes from those facts and in what it leaves out.
