## Also checked and found correct
- **CoactDetect bar rebuild (Fig 4):** bar = null mean + z*·sd, with sd = (obs − mean)/z, which is equivalent to p ≤ α.

- **SCE:** 200 surrogates at bench and `detect_folder` (the 1000 default is never used in any path the page reports), 99th percentile, at least 3 ROIs, no merging.
