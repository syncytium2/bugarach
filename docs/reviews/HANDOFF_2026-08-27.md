# Murderboard run — HANDOFF.md (the integrated handoff, 2026-08-27)

## What was at stake

Two sessions closed together and the next one starts cold on a big push. This file is the
only thing standing between it and re-deriving two days of work — and it is not a document
that happens to sit at the root, it **is** the repo's "something is in flight" signal.
`session_briefing.sh` reads its opening at every session start, and
`tests/test_handoff_is_honest.py` fails when every PR it names has closed.

That made the sharpest finding possible: **the guard rejected the handoff.**

`test_every_pr_the_handoff_claims_is_still_open` went red on the first draft. The opening
forty lines — the region the guard checks and the briefing shows — named exactly one PR,
**#298**, which is closed. The file asserted *"five PRs are open"* in prose while the only
machine-checkable thing in its checked region was a dead one. A reader would have been
fine; the guard was not, and the guard was right. The open PRs are named in the opening
now, and it resolves all six live: **#50, #53, #270, #292, #304 open, #298 closed.**

That is the same defect this handoff's closing section is about, caught on the handoff
itself: a claim true in prose and unverifiable where it counted.

**Role 1 caught the other one.** The draft quoted the two-hook total as `8,360B + 7,028B =
15,388B`. Re-measured at review time: **8,962B + 5,817B**. The figure had moved overnight,
inside the same document's lifetime, exactly as the todo it points at predicts. It now
gives a rounded ~15KB, says the number moves, and sends the reader to `docs/todo/` for the
live value — which is the rule `docs/handoffs/README.md` gained yesterday, applied to the
first page written after it.

## The file starved the decisions it was written to surface

The full suite, run after the roles, failed on
`test_it_surfaces_work_that_is_finished_and_waiting_on_a_person`. `ALARM_ROOM` is a
**shared pool spent in emission order**, and a root handoff renders first — correctly, it
is the *stop, something is in flight* line. This one is 601B, which left the
waiting-on-Tony list too little, and the third item's action line came out as
`-> Did […]`.

The third item is *"did you mean to close PR #298"* — one of the three decisions the page
exists to put in front of a person. **The handoff was cutting the decision list to make
room for itself.**

That is the trade `session_briefing.sh`'s own `ALARM_ROOM` comment already calls backwards:
truncating the one block with a person waiting at the end of it, to protect something else.
The fix follows its logic — the handoff is the safer thing to shorten, because its alarm
line says READ THE FILE and the reader is about to open it, while a decision request
appears nowhere but the briefing. `emit_bounded` takes a **reserve**: a block that renders
early is told how much a later, less truncatable one needs, and spends only the remainder.
The `waiting:` line on the ADR todo was also cut from 278B to 160B — three sentences where
the convention is one.

**A second test was written for this and deleted.** It counted action lines, and the
truncation cut the *tail* of the third line rather than the line itself, so the count never
moved and the test passed with the fix reverted. `test_it_surfaces_work_...` already
asserts the real property and fails without the reserve — verified in both directions. A
guard nobody has watched fail is not a guard, and shipping a redundant one that cannot fire
would have been this session's own criticism, committed.

**The budget could not simply be raised.** `tools/hook_spill_census.sh` — built by the
other session from 55 real spill records — puts the threshold at **(8,962B, 10,186B]** and
rates the 9,000B budget *"ok, 1186B under the smallest refusal."* There is no room to buy;
the content had to give.

## Then CI refused it, for the same reason one layer down

The suite passed locally and `merge_when_green` still declined: **test (3.11) failed.**

Reproduced in a throwaway clone rather than waited for. On a machine with no
`core.hooksPath`, no machine-local board and no darkroom, every standing alarm fires at
full length instead of collapsing to `commit gates: ACTIVE` and `darkroom: -> …`. The
briefing there came to **9,078B against the 9,000B budget** — over, so the ladder degraded
`FOUNDATIONS §9` to its six bolded claims. This handoff's 601B opening was the 78B that
tipped it.

That is `10555df`'s lesson repeating on the page written after it: *"measured on a
configured machine, where the standing alarms collapse to one line each."* The opening was
cut from ten lines to six — a handoff's first fourteen lines are an **alarm**, not a
document, and the document is right underneath. Fresh clone is **8,944B** now and the
configured machine 8,578B; the page quotes the fresh number, because that is the one that
binds and the one CI runs.

The budget could not absorb it either: `hook_spill_census.sh` rates 9,000B as *"ok, 1186B
under the smallest refusal"* of 10,186B, so the census's own safety rule leaves about 186B
of room to buy and the shortfall was 78B before the trim and 56B of headroom after it.

**Three verification layers, three different failures, none visible to the one before it:**
the roles caught a closed PR in the checked region, the full suite caught the decision list
being starved, and CI caught the fresh-clone budget. Each was a real defect in this page.

## What would validate this

Everything checkable in the page was re-derived at review time rather than carried from
the sessions that produced it: suite **1,391 collected**, `main` at **ab1dbfd**, **72**
open todos, **4** ACTIVE board claims, `docs/RESET.md` **absent from main**, #270 **red**
(3/3 FAILURE), the `parity-was-the-inheritance` branch alive at **016df3b**, and the site
**10 commits behind** with one change to a served page. The guard evidence — 7 of 39
recordings above 0.38 crowding, +0.071 recall in the `<10 s` bin over 17 of 24 seeds
against a control matched on both margins — was checked against
`docs/reviews/guard_prior_art_2026-08-26.md` rather than restated from memory.

A third check exists that no previous handoff had: with this file at the root the briefing
delivers **8,962B against a 9,000B budget**. That is 38 bytes of headroom, and the page now
says so in terms, because the ladder's next rung degrades `FOUNDATIONS §9` to its claims.

## How it generalises

The page's own closing argument is that every defect in two days was a **status** defect —
true when written, false when read. This review found two more of exactly that kind in the
page making the argument. The countermeasure that worked, again, was not care: it was that
a test and a canary were watching. The handoff names a PR a test can resolve; the briefing
prints its own size. **Both findings here came from running something, not from reading.**
