**Thresholds used** (project conventions, tuned for a scrolling Markdown report rather than slides, and stated so they can be argued with): a prose block over **120 words** without a figure or table between it and the next one; **two or more consecutive prose-only blocks** carrying quantitative claims; a figure-bearing document under **~20% figure share of rendered canvas**; a results section with **no figure**. Slide thresholds (40 words/slide, 50% canvas) do not transfer to a report, so I substituted these and flag that substitution itself as a judgment call.
