## The first sentence I could not follow

Three unknowns in one clause: **"the budget"** is at first use here and is not glossed until §2 ("the shared false-alarm budget" — budget of what, set by whom, shared between whom?); **"the answer"** has no referent anywhere on the page; **"shortfall"** is never defined on the page at all (I had to reverse-engineer it from §4's "−0.032 as run, −0.018 tuned (44%…)" to learn it means the as-run deficit against CoactDetect, closed toward zero).
