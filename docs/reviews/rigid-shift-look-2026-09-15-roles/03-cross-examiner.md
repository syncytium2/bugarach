# Cross-Examiner (role 3), round 1: consistency findings for `docs/learned/rigid_shift_look/README.md`

Nearly all of the note's numbers match the run outputs. The problems are in how those numbers are described: which displacement a value belongs to, the thresholds the note draws but does not apply the same way everywhere, and terms the glossary already defines differently.

## Checked and consistent (no finding)

- **Lab counts:** 84 recordings, 44 mice and 1,501 window pairs per stream. The per-group pair counts (DI 306, MALE 392, ORX 447, OVX 356) add up to 1,501. The recording-identity note's group sizes (25 + 22 + 20 + 17) add up to 84.
- **Twins:** 31 ROIs and 20 twin pairs per participation level on the lab folder; 566 ROIs, 12,634 frames at 0.11792 s and 10 twin pairs on Cossart.
- **Planted events:** planted jitter of one frame matches `surrogate_stats.destruction_twins`. The assessor's null is a circular shift (`assess.py`).
- **K scaling:** 55, 73, 110 and 146 ROIs equal the lab K of 3, 4, 6 and 8 ROIs scaled by 566/31.
- **Figure 3 values:** 0.549, 0.563, lower bounds 0.509 and 0.510, and the group values at 44.8 s. Counts stay within 0.4 %.
- **Figure 4 values:** 0.41, 0.16, 0.07, 0.04 on fast and 0.60, 0.29, 0.13, 0.07 on slow.
- **Figures 5 and 6 values:** every Cossart leak, count and destruction value quoted.
- **Arithmetic:** 113 and 283 ROIs are 20 % and 50 % of 566; 28 ROIs per bin; 25 minutes.
- **The 5 s and 5.6 s cells that appear in both lab runs:** leak and count values are identical. Destruction point estimates are identical; the 95 % intervals differ in the fourth decimal because they were resampled.
- **DI claim:** "DI least stationary" matches `recording_identity.md`.
- **Legends:** figure legends name the same displacements as the metadata files and `J_SEC` in the runner.
- **Axis labels:** the 1,501 / 44 / 84 and 1,257 / 32 / 59 labels match the tables.
