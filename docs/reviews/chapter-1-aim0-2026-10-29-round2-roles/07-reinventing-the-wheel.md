## Checked and matching the reference (no finding)

- **Predictions use the tool's own estimator.** `theory.predict_solve` sends predicted (f2, f3) through `mcr.solve` (fixed model), so it has the same refusal when f2 or f3 is not positive. DT and WINDOWS come from `mcr`.
- **Chance-cluster rate.** `theory.chance_clusters_per_min` reads `bin_width_sec` and `merge_bins` from the signature of `assess_coactivity`. The factor 60 q (1 − q)^merge_bins matches the run-start rule in `_clusters` (a new group starts when the gap to the previous triggered bin exceeds `merge_bins`, i.e. 2). q = binom.sf with p = 1 − e^(−b·bin) uses the same p as `measure_chance_floor.closed_forms` and `probe_field_size.binom_sf`. Recomputing the tail with scipy rather than importing `probe_field_size.binom_sf` is harmless.
- **The grid itself.** `aim1_one_source.py` measures with the shipped `assess_coactivity` (K = 3, 4, 6; 1 s bins; merge 2; 120 surrogates, below the shipped default of 1000, which the record states) and with `mcr.measure`. `held_out.py` reuses `a1.cell` and `a1.chance_cell`, moving only the seeds.
- **The general form's surrogate.** The surrogate model in `event_train._coefficients` matches `mcr.shifted`: distinct offsets of `SURR_SPACING · w` per cell, and a random permutation plus a common shift that leave the expectation unchanged. The empirical train uses `simulate._place_renewal` with the grid's min_sep and interval_cv = 1.
- **Count detector.** `count_detect`'s 3 s merge gap joins exactly one uncalled 2 s bin, so m = 2 holds for both tools, as §1.7 states. The figure uses the shipped `_coact_count` and `count_detect` directly.
- **Membership.** `membership_limit.py` calls `stat_dispersion`, `pvalues_margin`, `pvalues_uniform` and `AssemblyResult.verdict()` (Bonferroni across the two statistics). Figure 1.6's "both" and "either" map to "structure-beyond-rate" and "not no-assembly".
- **Event floor.** The 6 and 12 cells come from `event_floor.window_floor`, as recorded in `separation/assessor_split/derivation.md`.
- **Small duplicates that match exactly.** `like_for_like.null_rate` copies the null rate in `theory.summary` (both use ddof = 0). `split.py` `_mround` is equivalent to `matlab_round` for non-negative onsets.
