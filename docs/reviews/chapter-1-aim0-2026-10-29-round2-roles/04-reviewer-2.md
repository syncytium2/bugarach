## Verdict

I would not sign. Two framing claims in the chapter are not supported by its own evidence:
- that theory explains both brute-force failures;
- what the joint fit can do "at realistic values".

Several comparisons that carry the headline claims are not like-for-like. These include joint fit against the existing tools, and likelihood tests against the binning tools. Each one is tilted toward the conclusion.
