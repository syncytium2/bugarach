# Role 8, naive-reader accessibility: review of `docs/learned/tube_self_supervised/README.md`

I read the page as a stranger, using only the page, its four figures and `docs/GLOSSARY.md`. I opened no review records, handoffs or todos. The reader I had in mind knows calcium imaging but did not run this experiment. A term counts as defined only if the page defines it. A glossary entry does not count, because the page never points the reader to the glossary.

**The short version:** a stranger is lost before the first figure. The top summary uses about 15 terms before they are defined. A section listing what changed comes next and repairs things the reader has not seen yet. Two figures also tell the reader something the text contradicts.
