# Methods review of `docs/learned/tube_self_supervised/README.md`

**Scope:** worktree `<worktree>`. Line numbers below refer to the artifact. I made no edits. The only scratch files are extracted PDF text under `.../scratchpad/mb/`.

**Summary.** The code mostly matches the report. The main problems are two misreadings of the surrogate papers, a claim that the Figure 1 features cannot see alignment between ROIs (the report's own slow-stream result contradicts it), a stronger statement about window-edge detections than the report makes, and several scoring quirks that go unreported.

| 1 | L355–357, L409–411 (Stella 2022; whole-train shifting) | The published method shifts each **trial** separately. ## What I checked and found correct

- **Elephant surrogates:** `dither_spike_train(edges=True)` drops onsets pushed past the edge, and `edges=False` clamps them to the edge rather than wrapping, so "Elephant does not wrap either" is right. `rigid_frames` builds the same shift as `surrogates.rigid_shift`: `floor(k+0.5+u)`, u uniform in [−J, J), leavers dropped. Only the seeding differs, and the equivalence test passes.
- **Drop rate:** rigid shift drops 0.53 % of onsets at 10 s and 1.08 % at 20 s.
- **Classifier and bootstrap:** the classifier is a linear forced choice with no intercept, and folds are grouped by mouse. The bootstrap resamples mice and refits 1,000 times, and a mouse drawn twice stays in one fold. There are 1,501 window pairs per stream.
- **Windows and positive control:** interior windows exclude the first and last 60 s, so onsets dropped at the edges never enter the leak features. The dither positive control's lowest interval bound is 0.699.
- **Objective and head input:** the objective matches the text (crop 4,096, top 1 % = 40 frames, `softplus(shift − real)`, 900 steps, batch 3). The forward pre-hook captures the head input without modifying it.
- **Scoring and thresholds:** pooled F1 goes through `pool_scores`, and probe firings are excluded from precision. The truth-reading threshold is picked by `pick_threshold` with its edge guard, and sits on a grid edge in 4 of 360 fits, all `line`.
- **Bake-off statistics:** the *t* values and "without the largest fold" figures reproduce.
- **Plant probe:** the burst spans 6 frames (0.6 s), the fuzz spans 2.9 s, ink is equal, and the ⚠ test uses the population SD over 12 fields.
- **Citations:** Louis 2010 does recommend whole-train dithering (their "tr-di") and credits Pipa 2008 and Harrison &amp; Geman 2009. Pipa 2008 does cite the multiple-shift method (Grün 1999).
