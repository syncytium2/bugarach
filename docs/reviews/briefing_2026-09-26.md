# Murderboard run: the full-panel night briefing

**What was at stake.** Tony asked for one page he could click and be briefed from, top down: the
leaderboard first, the new bench beside the old, then examples on real recordings, then the
rasters. The page is read to decide what the night's search and training changed. So a count on
it that overstates, or an example that shows the opposite of its label, would be read as the
result.

**What the review found, in the order it mattered.**

1. **The headline measured the reference, not the rows.** On the new bench most rows beat
   CoactDetect at its shipped setting: 12 of 21 fast rows, 16 of 18 slow, 12 of 20 combined. Against
   CoactDetect's own new-bench proposal, 0, 1 and 1 do. The new bench mostly shows that the shipped
   setting loses:
   - fast: decoys become a larger share of its calls (the same rate per seed, half the events);
   - slow and combined: it merges close events and loses recall.

   The page now leads with that sentence (roles 4, 5, 8, 11).
2. **Counting mistakes.** A row that repeats another's scores on the new bench had also been
   dropped on the old bench, where it doesn't repeat. Fast's old bench read 3 of 21; it is 4 of 22
   (roles 1, 3, 6).
3. **One example showed the opposite of its label.** Its call sat exactly 2.5 s from CoactDetect's.
   It was classed "only the leader" through floating-point error, although the scorer's own test
   counts that gap as agreement. The examples are now drawn from calls clear of the tolerance, and
   each table prints the gap distribution (roles 1, 4, 6, 8).
4. **The section 2 comparison was not like for like.** CoactDetect cannot call below each window's
   event floor. The learned leader has no floor, and on slow only 44 of its 213 calls that
   CoactDetect lacks reach it. The page now says so and counts the calls that reach the floor
   (role 4).
5. **The page had its own rule for search limits.** Round 2 had exempted a setting stopped at a hard
   limit (the count offset at 0) as "by design". The search's own code marks those settings not
   adoptable, and no ADR says otherwise. The page now follows the search, and the top unflagged row
   moved:
   - fast: count (binned) proposal → LoCo · shipped;
   - slow: count (sliding) · starting point → line pick (roles 6, 7).
6. **A third of the viewer links could not open.** The viewer has no combined stream. Combined
   recordings now carry no viewer link and point to the review pages instead (role 8).

**What would validate it.** An independent pass on the repaired page (hash 2c97f528), which this run
did not do, because it stopped at the round cap. See the residual list below.

**How this generalises.** Two of the six findings were the page writing its own rule where the
project already had one: the search's bracketing verdict and the scorer's inclusive tolerance. The
cheapest defense was reading the canonical code's verdict rather than re-deriving it.
