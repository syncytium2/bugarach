## Checked, and correct (no finding)

- **Paired difference.** It matches what the page says. For each seed, F1 is pooled over that seed's quiet and busy recordings. ΔF1 is the mean over seeds of (row − CoactDetect shipped). The interval is a percentile bootstrap over seeds, 2,000 draws. `n_dropped` = 0 for every row on the page; only non-picked chorus seeds drop any. Verified: yes.
- **"Seeds per background" counts.** New bench: fast 48, slow 24, combined 24. Old bench: 24 on each stream. These are right: `seeds_by_bench` [first, last] gives last − first + 1, `seed_factor` doubles fast under realistic spacing only, and each seed makes one recording per background. Verified: yes.
- **"Old bench" means spacing `bench`.** `build()` refuses a file with the wrong spacing; the ORX files are skipped. Verified: yes.
- **Pairing across files.** The count files carry their own CoactDetect rows. Their `coact:shipped` and `coact:proposal` rows equal the night's to every digit (F1, calls per hour, ΔF1) on both benches and all three streams, so the recordings are the same and the pairing holds. Verified: yes.
- **Budgets.** `fresh_budgets` applies the same probe, null, elevated-outside-quiet and precision-swing tests as `make_admissible`. The crowded budget is not shown, which is correct: ADR-0010 part 4 retires `MAX_CROWDED_DROP`. Verified: yes.
- **Count detectors' own budgets.** `count` and `count_sliding` have entries in all three dicts on all three benches. Combined `count_sliding` precision limit is 0.15; learned models are held to CoactDetect's. Verified: yes.
- **New-bench claim.** The proposals and picks were chosen on the new bench: every search.json says `spacing: realistic`, and training ran with `--spacing realistic`. The pick definition in Terms matches `picked()`: 5 seeds, CoactDetect's null budget. Verified: yes.
- **Example variants.** `read_calls` keeps `own_floor` for the floored four and `unfloored` for rate, locust and chorus, so each detector has one variant. This matches ADR-0010 part 6 ("runs as it is defined and scored") and the review run. Verified: yes.
- **`ran_on_real`.** It matches the review run's `chosen` block on every stream, including slow `chorus_gain_norm_part` being absent (no pick). Verified: yes.
