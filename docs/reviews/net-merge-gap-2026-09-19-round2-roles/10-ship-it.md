## Checks that passed, explicitly

- **Text overflow / clipping**: zero `<text>` bboxes outside any viewBox, at both 1100 px and 400 px. The nine bbox "overlaps" the detector reported are all consecutive-line leading (2 px vertical, 11 px font on 13 px lines) or the 0.8 px between a dot's count and the ×n below it; at 3× magnification no glyphs touch (`c_fig2_countsA.png`, `c_fig3_gutterB.png`, `c_fig4_gutterA.png`).
- **11 px floor**: honoured everywhere. SVG text is 11/12/13 px only; the smallest HTML text is 14 px (`table`, `code`). No element in `main` computes under 11 px at either width.
- **Contrast**: `.dim` #444 (9.7:1), figcaption #222, SVG note ink #595959 (7.0:1). No grey-on-grey text.
- **Axis labels and units**: every axis names the quantity — "merge gap (s), the eight grid values, evenly spaced" (with the explicit warning that it is not a time axis), "net minus CoactDetect, held-out F1", "held-out F1, mean over folds". No placeholder like "value". F1 is dimensionless and the pages says so in words.
- **Shared y-limits / shared ranges**: Figure 1 A and B share 0.66–0.82; Figures 3/4 share their x range between panels. (The *scale* does not match — finding 1.)
- **Panel lettering**: A/B on every multi-panel figure, and neither the captions nor the prose refer to panels by "left"/"right". The spatial words that do occur ("right of the dashed zero line", "left of the axis", "beyond its left end") describe the axis, not a panel.
- **Figure numbering**: 1, 2, 3, 4 with no gaps; every caption opens `Figure N.`; the table is labelled `Table 1.`
- **No vertical lines or bars annotating a histogram** — no histograms on the page.
- **Document properties name this file**: `<html lang="en">`, `<title>The nets' merge gap, tuned</title>` matching the h1, `meta description/generator/author/date` all specific to this page and dated 2026-09-19. No template residue.
- **Accessibility scaffolding**: all five scroll regions are `role="region" tabindex="0"` with a distinct `aria-label`, and `.wide:focus` has a visible 2 px outline; all four SVGs are `role="img"` with a descriptive `aria-label` (no `<title>` child, which is acceptable given the label).
- **Darkroom copy is in sync**: `<darkroom>/bugarach/2026-09-18-fair-comparison-run/report/merge-gap.html`, 22:12:25, 81,188 B against the repo's 81,189 B — the one-byte difference is the sibling link (`index.html` vs `report.html`), and `index.html` is present beside it.
