## The first sentence I could not follow

**The lede's first sentence**: *"The gap the nets were never allowed to tune is worth about one noise unit, and the check that bounds it refused a wider one every time."*

Why I lost it, cold, clause by clause: "the gap" — which gap? (the title says "merge gap" but the word *merge* has not appeared in a sentence yet, and merge gap is not defined until §1). "one noise unit" — a unit invented in this sentence, with no value; I have to reach the second sentence to learn it is 0.010 F1, and §7 to learn what it measures. "the check that bounds it" — bounds *what*, the gap or the gap's worth? The check is not named here. "refused a wider one" — a wider *check* or a wider *gap*? The nearest noun is "check". I had to read §2 and §5 and come back before this sentence parsed.

Runner-up, and the harder one: **§2 final sentence** — *"And the check is soft where it is applied: a choice's crowded cost moves by a median 0.002 F1 and as much as 0.045 between the inner fits the check is enforced on and the outer refits that produce the reported number, against a limit of 0.02."* "Soft" is a relative word with no referent; it turns out to mean "the quantity the check tests is itself unstable under resampling", which is not what "soft" says. It also needs *choice*, *crowded cost*, *inner fits* and *outer refits*, none of which are defined before §5.

Third: **§1** — *"…and the report's ranking rests on it not being."* The trailing ellipsis ("not being **the nets' handicap**") is a garden path; I re-read it three times.
