# Murderboard run: the final-parameters report

## What was at stake

`docs/learned/runs/2026-09-25-final-parameters/README.md` is the morning report of the
final-parameters night. It asks Tony nine decisions, the first of them whether to adopt four detector
settings into `bench*.py`. A wrong reason for a gain, or a budget pass that could not have failed, goes
straight into what ships. Three rounds ran, the cap: one full review and two blind ones, each with all
eleven roles.

## What was found

**Round 1** checked the tables against the records and found the report had been written process-first.
It was reordered to put decisions first. **Round 2** (blind) found that the report said two sets of
fresh intervals "barely touch" when they do not. It also found the guard-cap account wrong, that
slow locust's budget failure came from where the elevated-rate test now sits and not from the floor,
and that the headline comparison lacked a paired test. The fix for the last one was
`tools/final_parameters_review_checks.py`. All four paired fresh-seed gains came out above zero.

**Round 3** (blind) found the remaining substantive errors, all now repaired on the page:

- **The stated reason for the gains was wrong** (Reviewer 2 and Cross-Examiner, blocking; Prove It and
  RTFM, major). The page said most of each gain was fewer calls on decoys. The record shows otherwise.
  The gains sit on the busy background, F1 without decoy calls rises as much or more, and decoy calls
  barely move. Recall falls under three of the four.
- **Three locust proposals carried an earlier "not a setting to ship" flag the page dropped**
  (Reviewer 2, blocking). The flag comes from the open locust anchor question. Slow locust also passes
  the elevated-rate test by construction: 25.6 s between calls caps it at 2.34 calls per minute.
- **The SPIKE-synch naming ruling may be avoidable** (RTFM). A rerun puts the whole gain in
  `C_threshold`. The fixed window adds nothing, because the 0.25 s cap binds for over 94% of onsets
  either way.
- **Decision 2 used overlapping intervals where a paired test is needed** (RTFM). The paired rerun
  shows fast's binned SCE is better than combined's own on the combined bench, +0.024 [+0.005, +0.044].
- **Other substantive corrections.**
  - Binned SCE's elevated-rate check is live, not powerless.
  - "Don't care" events can still take calls away from counted events in the scorer.
  - Decision 7 can change today's set (combined CoactDetect).
  - Choosing on the fresh seeds spends them.
  - Both options in Decision 3 turn failures into passes.
  - The real-data section omitted high K⁺ and TTX, the settings used, the nesting of group in imaging
    day, and the second reading of the floor difference.
  - Two numbers (1.83 and 5.00 calls per minute; 142 of 144 stable floors) came from a round-2
    reviewer's own measurement and are now cited to it.

**This run did not converge.** Round 3 found three blocking issues, all repaired, and the repaired page
has not had a fourth blind pass. Per the process, it is delivered unconverged at the cap.
