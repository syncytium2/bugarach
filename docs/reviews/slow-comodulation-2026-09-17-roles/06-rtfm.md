# Methods review ("RTFM") of the slow co-modulation explainer, first round

The shipped arithmetic is right: the pair count, the chance formula, the rigid shift and the circular shift all do what the code says. Four findings are major. Each is about what a number means, not about a coding bug, and each can be checked in the data. The page's headline claims about the long-lag shoulder, the Cossart shoulder, the slow-stream block control and Figure 4 need them fixed first.

**How I grounded it.** The lit-cache tool is not vendored here, so I fetched sources by hand into `.../scratchpad/review/r6/`:

## Checked and sound (no finding)

- **The fast autocorrelation.** Padding to at least 2L gives the non-circular sum with no wrap, and the brute-force test agrees. The circular-shift arm reads 0.00 ± 0.01 in every bin of all three folders. That is the empirical check that the chance formula E(k) = (S² − Σn_i²)(L−k)/L² matches what the observed sum counts: ordered pairs, lags k ≥ 0, each unordered onset pair at a non-zero lag counted once.
- **The identity from Brody's integral rule, reproduced exactly.** On lab fast, the total excess over all lags is 4,596 pairs. Half the zero-lag excess is 9,193 / 2 = 4,596.5.
- **`rigid_frames`.** One offset per ROI, uniform in [−J, J), rounded, with onsets past the ends dropped. That is Elephant's `dither_spike_train` with `edges=True`. The 20 s trim does guarantee that no dropped onset enters: the largest shift is round(J/dt), which is never more than ceil(20/dt), the trim. I checked this at every dt in the folders, including 0.11848 s, where both are 169 frames.
- **The block control's boundaries.** They are fixed and independent of the data, as a valid interval-jitter null requires, and each ROI's per-block counts are kept exactly (tested).
- **CoactDetect settings.** The fast parameters equal `bench.OPERATING_POINTS['coact']`. The slow parameters equal the explore_sce slow point in the `coact_detect` docstring. Every onset is removed on a closed span [onset, onset + width], which matches the span `coact_detect` returns.
- **Holes left by removing episodes.** Episodes cover a median 0.3 % (fast) and 0.2 % (slow) of the time. As a check, I removed the same spans from circularly shifted trains: that reads about 0 beyond the first bin (+0.013 and +0.066 in it). This is not a meaningful artefact.
- **The lab fast shoulder is not events.** Applying the block control after removing CoactDetect episodes still leaves +0.07 at 3.7–28 s, against +0.08 without the removal.
