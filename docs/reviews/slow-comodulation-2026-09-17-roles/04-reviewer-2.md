# Adversarial review of the slow co-modulation page, first round

**What I checked:** the README; all four PNGs, reading the images themselves; `summary.json`; the committed `tools/measure_slow_comodulation.py` and its tests; the per-recording `results.json`; the goal page; `simulate.py` and the bench's `hot_window`; and the other session's rigid-shift branch.

I ran three kinds of computation:
- **Bootstraps over mice:** paired differences, re-run over imaging days as well.
- **Per-recording checks:** the weight each recording carries in the pooled curves, and how the excess spreads across recordings.
- **Seven synthetic test worlds:** run against a snapshot of the committed tool.

**Scratch files:** my only file is that snapshot, at `<scratchpad>/review/r4/committed/`. I killed two earlier simulation attempts; they failed at worker start-up and produced no numbers.

**The tool and tests are being edited while I review.** `git status` shows uncommitted changes to both (a new `count_variance`, an `rng` helper in place of `seed31`). All my reruns used the committed version.
