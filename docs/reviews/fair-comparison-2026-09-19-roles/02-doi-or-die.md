## Where each trace stopped
- **Nadeau & Bengio.** Read in full, both the 1999 conference version and the 2003 journal version.
  - **Backward:** the correction starts with them. They cite Dietterich (1998), Neural Computation 10(7):1895–1923, doi:10.1162/089976698300017197, for the *problem* (the paired t across resamples is too liberal), not for the correction. Their 1999 CIRANO technical report was not reached.
  - **Forward:** Bouckaert & Frank 2004, PAKDD, Lecture Notes in Computer Science (LNCS) 3056:3–12, doi:10.1007/978-3-540-24775-3_3 — read in full. It names the "corrected repeated k-fold cv test". Bengio & Grandvalet 2004, JMLR 5:1089–1105 — abstract only. Corani & Benavoli 2015, Machine Learning 100(2):285–304 — search summary only. They use Nadeau and Bengio's ρ = n_test/n_total in a correlated Bayesian t-test and are the nearest modern restatement for k-fold.
