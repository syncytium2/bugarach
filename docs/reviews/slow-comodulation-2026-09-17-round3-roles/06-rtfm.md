# Methods review of the slow co-modulation page and its analysis code, commit 2f77de1

Two claims on the page are wrong as written. The per-pair comparison between the Dard et al. dataset and the lab folder rests on a denominator that counts ROIs with no onsets. The page also says about a third of the recorded excess is a straight-line trend, but the run's own synthetic check shows a trend-free world loses the same third to detrending.

Both problems were found with the tool's own functions, applied to the same export folders and to `results.json`. Scratch work is in `<scratchpad>/review3/r6/`. I read these sources: Schluter 1984 (the scanned reprint, equations 1–5), Brody 1999 (equation 3.6 and footnote 6), and the Amarasingham et al. 2012 supplement for the definition of interval jitter.

## Checked and found correct

- **Pair counts:** `pair_counts` handles ordered and unordered pairs correctly, counts zero lag once in both the observed and expected counts, and uses the correct independence expectation, Σ_{i≠j} nᵢnⱼ (L−k)/L².
- **Brody 1999 analogy:** his eq. 3.6 matches the single-window zero-sum claim.
- **Block control:** the interval-jitter description matches Amarasingham et al. 2012. `window_circular_shift` keeps each ROI's count per block, including the short last block.
- **Rigid shift:** `rigid_frames` spreads an event's coincidences over about ±2*J*, as the page says.
- **Episode removal:** onsets are removed by the same half-open bins CoactDetect uses, with identical settings on both streams. Detection runs on the untrimmed window and the result is trimmed afterwards.
- **Nulls:** `NULL_OF` is applied consistently in `pooled`, the per-recording checks and the paired checks. The detrended arithmetic (residual sum of squares ÷ (n−2), raw variance with ddof = 1) is correct, and so is the Kish formula.
- **Quoted numbers:** every pooled, paired, per-recording, synthetic and removal-share number I compared against `summary.json` matches it.
