Nothing here is a recording of anything. Event times are drawn from the model in bugarach.simulate, re-implemented for this page — same model, a different generator, so a seed reproduces in this browser and does not match Python's numbers.

The settings you land on are measured, not chosen. Rate, ROI count, participation and jitter come off 84 untreated slices, and every one of them used to be a guess that made coordination easier than it is — six ROIs with a third of a second of spread, not half the field firing together. The old guesses are on the switches, and worth a look.

The windows are labels, not effects. The statistics are identical inside and outside every one of them. Simulating a treatment would spend the effect the experiment exists to measure — so the drug is a name on the axis and nothing more.

Assess coordinationno detector involved
How much coordination is here, measured against a rate-matched null. Each ROI's own train is circularly shifted by its own random lag, which holds its rate and its burstiness and destroys only cross-ROI phase — so what survives is coordination beyond what the recording's rate explains.

K is a scan, not a choice. The floor for how many ROIs make an event moves the headline by an order of magnitude, so every K is shown and none is marked as the answer. Quoting one of these numbers means naming its K.

Baseline only. Coordination properties are not taken from a treatment — that is what the instrument is pointed at, and sourcing them from it assumes the answer. A recording with no declared regions is assessed whole, and it says so.
Detect eventsRateDetect
RateDetect, the same port the rest of this project cites. It flags moments when the population rate rises above its own local context, and it draws no random numbers — so this browser copy is checked against the Python one exactly, to 1e-9, on every event it finds. A mistake here cannot pass as sampling noise.

One of six, and the settings are not calibrated for your recordings. These are the values the project benches at. Finding the right ones for a preparation is what the simulate step above is for: measure, simulate from what you measured, tune against planted ground truth, then point the tuned instrument at everything.
